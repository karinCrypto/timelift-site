FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it place a real phone call?

No. It is an in-app screen that looks like an incoming call. No call charges and no phone number needed.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign up in about a minute with Google, Apple or email. Your plans and records then follow you across devices.

Is my voice or meeting audio stored?

AI chat and meeting notes are sent to AI providers only to produce the answer, and are not stored on our server. We ask for consent before your first AI chat.

Is this an ADHD treatment app?

No. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything. It is a planner that helps with planning and habits.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Android phones and Mac.
